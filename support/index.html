🔓 Unlocking perks in an app

⚡ GitHub Sponsors: automatic

In the app, open Settings → Support Fili → I'm a sponsor and sign in with GitHub. The app checks your sponsorship and unlocks your tier. It re-checks now and then, so the perks follow your sponsorship.

🔑 Any other method: a licence key

Pay with the reference above, or write to contact email with the app and the payment details. You'll receive a licence key by email. Paste it in Settings → Support Fili → Enter licence key. The app checks it offline: no account, no tracking.

Monthly support on Ko-fi, Liberapay or Buy Me a Coffee gets a key for a fixed period (e.g. 12 months), renewed on request.

🤖 Apps from Google Play

⚠️

Google doesn't allow apps on Google Play to unlock features with payments made elsewhere. So the Play versions have no ads and no tiers, and supporting from them doesn't unlock anything. The versions from GitHub Releases, F-Droid and itch.io have the perks.
